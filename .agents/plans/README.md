# Plans

This directory contains task-local implementation plans for work that benefits from explicit sequencing or risk analysis.

Create a Plan when a task is complex enough that a written execution approach will reduce uncertainty or coordination cost.

A Plan may contain:

- objective;
- relevant authority references;
- current understanding;
- proposed changes;
- validation plan;
- risks and unknowns;
- completion conditions.

Authority: task-local only.

Plans may become stale, may be rewritten, and may be deleted. They must not override accepted Specifications, Standards, ADRs, or current Architecture.

Suggested naming:

```text
issue-123-short-title.md
```

Starter template: `templates/plan.md`
